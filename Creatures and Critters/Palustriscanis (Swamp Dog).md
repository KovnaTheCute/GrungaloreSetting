Climate: Swamp

Domain: Multicellula
Kingdom: Spiriare
Phylum: Terraspiriare
Class: Capillus
Order: Violentusferus
Family: Venatorgregis
Genus: Palustriscanis

Diet: Primarily meat from larger animals

Description: Palustriscanis are partially arboreal and partially aquatic, breathing through lungs as a member of the Terraspiriare phylum. They have short snouts that allow them to completely close off their nostrils, being able to hold their breath for up to an hour at a time. They have webbed feet that they use for aquatic navigation along with strong and sharp claws that they use to climb and hunt. They have complex social calls and communicate information between packs regarding dangers, threats, food sources, and environment. Their primary food source is larger prey that packs work together to hunt down. There is significant gender dimorphism amongst this species, with males having much more vibrant webbing and fins while females are larger with more muted colors and more effective weapons. Females tend to be the ones to ambush prey due to their better ability to camouflage. The males use their bright colors to alert prey, corralling them into the ambush the females set up. Palustriscanis mate for life, spitting off from their packs to raise between 3 and 12 young. Packs require females to make kills and adult Palustriscanis kill young and reproduce more if there is a poor ratio, ideally aiming for 1 male for every three females.
