Climate: Desert

Domain: Multicellula
Kingdom: Spiriare
Phylum: Terraspiriare
Class: Pluma
Order: Caelumfulgur
Family: Caelumparalysis

Diet: Carnivore and Insectivore

Description: Caelumparalysis are nocturnal, with incredibly sharp vision they strike prey with devastating speed before swooping back up and waiting for their venom to take effect. Their venom is among the most venomous on the planet, with a LD50 of 0.01mg/kg they inject 100mg of venom per sting. Victims of their venom have all of their organs paralyzed, causing near instant death. They can reach speeds up to 100km/hr when diving and are extremely streamlined to reach such speeds. Caelumparalysis are highly territorial within their species, with the only toleration for another being to reproduce.

Caelumparalysis all share the same sex and are all capable of reproduction. When two meet, they engage in a lethal sparring contest using their venomous tails, the venom of each Caelumparalyis is unique to the individual, granting only a partial immunity to the same species. This partial immunity allows the loser of the dual to live, though it still partially paralyzes them. The victor then uses a specialized reproductive organ to inject the loser with half a dozen already fertilized eggs. After 2 weeks of gestation the eggs hatch within the Caelumparalysis and their first meal is the loser of the dual. The partially paralyzed Caelumparalysis is unable to produce any venom during this time and is lethargically slow. Luckily for it the only predators willing to risk the venom of a Caelumparalysis are desperate ones, and it has very little to worry about as it finds a sheltered space to slowly starve in while it waits for the eggs to hatch.
